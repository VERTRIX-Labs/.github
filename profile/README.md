## Who We Are

VERTRIX is an independent software development studio focused on designing and building digital solutions.

We believe in continuous learning, collaborative development and the use of modern technologies to transform ideas into functional products.

Our goal is to grow as a team of software developers while creating technology that makes a difference.

## What We Do

- **Web Development** — Modern and responsive web applications.
- **Software Engineering** — Maintainable and scalable software solutions.
- **Backend Development** — APIs, databases and server-side applications.
- **Infrastructure & Deployment** — Linux servers, VPS management and deployment.
- **AI-Assisted Development** — Integrating AI tools into modern development workflows.

## Technologies

**Backend:** Node.js, Express.

**Databases:** MySQL, PostgreSQL.

**Tools:** Git, GitHub, VS Code, Linux.
